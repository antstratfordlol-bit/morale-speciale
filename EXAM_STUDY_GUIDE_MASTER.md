## Examination Structure & Grading Rules

Prof. Pablo Requena structures the written examination into three distinct parts:

1. **Part 1: 20 Multiple-Choice Questions (*Scelta Multipla*)**
   - Assesses direct comprehension and textual retention of the official Magisterial documents.
   - Requires precise familiarity with paragraph numbers, technical definitions, and ethical distinctions.
2. **Part 2: 4 Short-Answer Questions (*Domande Brevi*)**
   - Requires concise, conceptually exact theological formulations (approx. 3–5 lines each).
   - Includes specific biblical loci (e.g. Genesis 9:6), encyclical sections (e.g. *EV* 99), and secular bioethical citations (e.g. Ruth Macklin in *BMJ* 2003).
   - **The "1 or 0" Rule:** For verbatim Magisterial definitions (such as direct abortion in *EV* 58 or euthanasia in *Iura et bona* II), full credit is awarded only for the precise, textual definition; inexact or descriptive approximations receive zero.
3. **Part 3: 1 Synthetic Essay Question (*Domanda da Rispondere in una Pagina*)**
   - A single-page development testing speculative synthesis, theological argumentation, and personalist criteria (e.g. moral evaluation of assisted reproductive technologies, or ethico-political debates on euthanasia).

# MODULE I: Instruction *Donum Vitae* (CDF, 22 February 1987)

*Instruction on Respect for Human Life in Its Origin and on the Dignity of Procreation: Replies to Certain Questions of the Day*  
*Primary Source File:* [`sources/magisterium/1987-donum-vitae.md`](file:///C:/Users/antst/Documents/20.%20STB/Year%203/Semester%20I/Morale%20Speciale/sources/magisterium/1987-donum-vitae.md)

---

### 1. Theological & Anthropological Framework
Published under the prefecture of Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger, *Donum vitae* addresses the ethical challenges precipitated by the emergence of In Vitro Fertilisation (IVF / FIVET, inaugurated with Louise Brown in 1978). The document is founded on two core pillars:
1. **The Inviolability of Innocent Human Life:** Human life is a gift of God (*donum vitae*) over which man has stewardship, not absolute dominion.
2. **The Truth of Conjugal Love:** Human procreation must be the bodily, personal fruit of the specific conjugal act of reciprocal spousal self-donation.

---

### 2. Verbatim Formulations to Learn by Heart

#### A. Moral Status of the Human Embryo (*Donum vitae*, I.1)
> **Italian:**  
> *"L'essere umano va rispettato e trattato come una persona fin dal suo concepimento e, perciò, da quello stesso momento gli si devono riconoscere i diritti della persona, tra i quali anzitutto il diritto inviolabile di ogni essere umano innocente alla vita."*
>
> **English:**  
> *"The human being must be respected and treated as a person from conception, and therefore from that same moment his rights as a person must be recognised, among which in the first place is the inviolable right of every innocent human being to life."*

- **Theological & Metaphysical Nuance:** Notice the precise formula: *"respect treated as a person"*. The Church does not enter into a philosophical definition of the exact temporal instant of animated ensoulment (*animatio*), but establishes an unconditional moral obligation: because modern biological science demonstrates that from the formation of the zygote a new, genetically distinct human individual is present with coordinated, autonomous development, the embryo must be accorded the full moral status and inviolable rights of a human person.

#### B. The Decisive Moral Criterion: Assistance vs Substitution (*Donum vitae*, II.B.7)
> **Latin:**  
> *"Si autem medium adhibitum subroget actum coniugalem, hoc medium moraliter non licitum est. Si contra medium adhibitum actum coniugalem adiuvet... moraliter licitum est."*
>
> **Italian:**  
> *"Se il mezzo tecnico facilita l'atto coniugale o lo aiuta a raggiungere i suoi obiettivi naturali, può essere moralmente accettato; se invece la procedura si sostituisce all'atto coniugale, essa è moralmente illecita."*
>
> **English:**  
> *"If the technical means facilitates the conjugal act or helps it to achieve its natural ends, it can be morally accepted; but if the procedure substitutes for the conjugal act, it is morally illicit."*

- **The *Adiuvet* vs *Subroget* Distinction:**
  - **Licit Assistance (*Adiuvet*):** Medical interventions that assist, accompany, or cure pathology in order to enable marital intercourse to attain fertilisation (e.g. hormonal therapy, surgical tubal clearance, NaProTechnology). The spouses remain the true personal and physical cause of procreation.
  - **Illicit Substitution (*Subroget*):** Medical procedures that replace the marital act (e.g. IVF/FIVET, ICSI, artificial insemination via masturbation). Procreation is transferred to the laboratory, subjecting the conceived child to technological domination (*dominium technicum*).

#### C. Inseparable Connection of Unitive and Procreative Meanings (*Donum vitae*, II.B.4)
> Building directly upon Paul VI's *Humanae vitae* (n. 12), the document affirms the *nexus inseparabilis*: the conjugal act possesses two intrinsic, divinely instituted meanings: the **unitive meaning** (*significatio unitiva*) and the **procreative meaning** (*significatio procreativa*). Just as contraception severs the procreative meaning from the unitive act, so artificial reproduction severs the unitive act from procreation. Both separations offend the truth of human love.

---

### 3. Comprehensive Breakdown by Parts & Questions

#### Part I: Respect for Human Embryos

1. **Prenatal Diagnosis (*Diagnosi Prenatale*, I.2):**
   - **Licit:** If it respects the life and integrity of the embryo and fetus, is directed toward his individual healing, and provides informed consent to the parents.
   - **Illicit:** If it is undertaken with the intention of procuring an abortion should a defect or disease be detected (*eugenic mentality*), or if it utilizes diagnostic methods that pose disproportionate risks to the unborn child. A woman who requests diagnosis with the intention of aborting commits a grave sin; any physician who encourages this is an accomplice.

2. **Therapeutic Interventions on Embryos (I.3):**
   - **Licit:** Only if they respect the life and integrity of the individual embryo, do not subject him to disproportionate risks, and are aimed directly at his healing, improvement of health, or individual survival.

3. **Research & Experimentation on Embryos (I.4):**
   - **Illicit:** Treating human embryos as mere experimental biological material or clinical disposable objects is a crime against their dignity.
   - Experimentation is licit *only* when there is moral certainty that it will not harm the child or the mother, and free parental consent has been given. Non-therapeutic research that risks the life or integrity of the embryo is intrinsically immoral.

4. **Use of Embryos Obtained *In Vitro* for Research (I.5):**
   - Producing human embryos *in vitro* for research or commercial purposes is gravely immoral.
   - Procuring or manipulating "surplus" embryos generated through IVF constitutes grave instrumentalisation.

5. **Manipulation, Freezing, and Hybrids (I.6):**
   - Any attempt to produce human-animal chimeras, hybridisation, ectogenesis (artificial gestation outside the uterus), or twin-fission cloning is intrinsically evil and violates human dignity.

---

#### Part II: Interventions upon Human Procreation

##### A. Heterologous Artificial Fertilisation (Donor Gametes / Surrogacy)
- **Moral Evaluation:** Gravely illicit (*actus intrinsece malus*).
- **Four Doctrinal Rationale Points:**
  1. **Violation of the Unity of Marriage:** Marriage confers upon spouses the exclusive right to become father and mother only through each other. Introducing third-party gametes (*sperm donor* or *egg donor*) violates marital fidelity.
  2. **Harm to the Child:** Denies the child his fundamental right to know his biological lineage, origin, and genealogy, creating a split between genetic, gestational, and social parentage.
  3. **Rupture of the Spousal Covenant:** Subverts family unity and introduces ontological alien elements into the spousal covenant.
  4. **Surrogate Motherhood (*Maternità surrogata*, II.A.3):** Intrinsically illicit. It treats the woman's body and maternal gestation as a commercial commodity, alienates the mother from her conceived child, and deprives the child of his right to be carried and nurtured by his mother.

##### B. Homologous Artificial Fertilisation (Husband and Wife)
- **Moral Evaluation of Homologous IVF (FIVET):** Gravely illicit (*actus intrinsece malus*).
- **Core Theological Arguments:**
  1. **Depersonalisation of Generation:** Replaces the personal self-gift of spousal intercourse with a mechanical laboratory procedure performed by biological technicians. The child is treated as a manufactured object (*productum technologicum*) under laboratory control (*dominium technicum*), rather than as a gift of personal love (*filius ut donum*).
  2. **Inherent Destruction of Embryos (*Perdita embrionaria*):** In vitro fertilisation routinely requires hyperovulation, the creation of supernumerary embryos, selective discard, and cryopreservation, thereby causing a mass loss of human lives.
  3. **Severing the *Nexus Inseparabilis*:** Procreation is achieved outside the marital act, breaking the sacred bond between love and life established by God.
- **Homologous Artificial Insemination (AIH, II.B.6):**
  - **Illicit:** If the sperm is obtained via masturbation and substitutes for marital intercourse.
  - **Licit:** If the technical device assists an already completed conjugal act (e.g. using a perforated non-spermicidal sheath to collect semen during natural marital intercourse and assisting its transit).

##### C. Pastoral Response to Infertility (II.B.8)
- Infertility is a heavy cross, but marriage does not confer a "right to have a child" (*non datur ius ad prolem*).
- A child is not a piece of property or a commodity to which spouses have an entitlement; a child is a person, a transcendent gift.
- Christian spouses facing infertility are called to authentic spiritual fruitfulness, foster care, legitimate adoption, educational initiatives, and works of charity.

# MODULE II: Instruction *Dignitas Personae* (CDF, 8 September 2008)

*Instruction on Certain Bioethical Questions*  
*Primary Source File:* [`sources/magisterium/2008-dignitas-personae.md`](file:///C:/Users/antst/Documents/20.%20STB/Year%203/Semester%20I/Morale%20Speciale/sources/magisterium/2008-dignitas-personae.md)

---

### 1. Continuity & Developments from *Donum Vitae*
Published twenty-one years after *Donum vitae* under Pope Benedict XVI and Cardinal William Levada, *Dignitas personae* updates Catholic bioethics in response to new biomedical advances: Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI), embryo cryopreservation, Pre-implantation Genetic Diagnosis (PGD), interceptive contraception, embryonic stem cell research, somatic versus germ-line gene therapy, cloning, and fetal cell lines.

---

### 2. Verbatim Formulations to Learn by Heart

#### A. The Three Fundamental Goods (*Dignitas personae*, n. 12)
> **Italian:**  
> *"Nel valutare la portata morale dei recenti sviluppi delle scienze mediche... le nuove tecniche mediche devono rispettare tre beni fondamentali:*  
> *a) il diritto alla vita e all'integrità fisica di ogni essere umano dal concepimento fino alla morte naturale;*  
> *b) l'unità del matrimonio, che comporta il reciproco rispetto del diritto dei coniugi a diventare padre e madre soltanto l'uno attraverso l'altro;*  
> *c) i valori specificamente umani della sessualità, che esigono che la procreazione debba essere perseguita come il frutto dell'atto coniugale specifico dell'amore tra gli sposi."*
>
> **English:**  
> *"In evaluating the moral dimension of recent developments in medical sciences... new medical techniques must respect three fundamental goods:*  
> *a) The right to life and physical integrity of every human being from conception to natural death;*  
> *b) The unity of marriage, which entails reciprocal respect for the right within marriage to become a father or mother only together with the other spouse;*  
> *c) The specifically human values of sexuality, which require that procreation be pursued as the fruit of the specific conjugal act of mutual spousal love."*

#### B. Moral Evaluation of Embryo Cryopreservation (*Dignitas personae*, nn. 18–19)
> **Italian:**  
> *"La crioconservazione è incompatibile con il rispetto dovuto agli embrioni umani: essa presuppone la loro produzione in vitro; li espone a gravi rischi di morte o di danno per la loro integrità fisica... li priva almeno temporaneamente dell'accoglienza e della gestazione materna; li pone in una situazione suscettibile di ulteriori offese e manipolazioni... Tutto ciò comporta che le migliaia di embrioni abbandonati determinano una situazione di ingiustizia di fatto insanabile."*
>
> **English:**  
> *"Cryopreservation is incompatible with the respect owed to human embryos: it presupposes their in vitro production; exposes them to grave risks of death or harm to physical integrity... deprives them at least temporarily of maternal reception and gestation; and places them in a situation susceptible to further offenses and manipulations... All this leads to the conclusion that the thousands of abandoned embryos represent an unjust situation which in fact cannot be resolved (*situazione di ingiustizia di fatto insanabile*)."*

#### C. Pre-implantation Genetic Diagnosis: PGD / *DGP* (*Dignitas personae*, n. 22)
> **Italian:**  
> *"La diagnosi pre-impianto è un'espressione della mentalità eugenetica che accetta l'aborto selettivo per impedire la nascita di bambini affetti da varie patologie... Tratta l'embrione umano come un semplice materiale di laboratorio."*
>
> **English:**  
> *"Pre-implantation genetic diagnosis is an expression of that eugenic mentality which accepts selective abortion in order to prevent the birth of children affected by various anomalies... It treats the human embryo as simple laboratory material."*

#### D. Interception and Contragestion (*Dignitas personae*, n. 23)
> **Technical Distinction:**
> - **Interceptive Methods (*Intercettivi*):** Interventions designed to prevent implantation of an already conceived blastocyst (e.g. morning-after pill, NorLevo, ellaOne, Intrauterine Device / IUD).
> - **Contragestive Methods (*Contragestivi*):** Interventions that provoke the detachment or expulsion of an already implanted embryo (e.g. RU-486 / mifepristone).
> - **Magisterial Verdict:** Anyone who seeks to prevent implantation or provoke expulsion directly wills abortion (*"chi desidera impedire l'annidamento di un embrione già concepito... persegue di fatto un aborto"*).

#### E. Biological Material of Illicit Origin & Refusal of Independence (*Dignitas personae*, nn. 34–35)
> **Rejection of the "Criterion of Independence":**  
> *"In questo senso, non è sufficiente il criterio di indipendenza formulato da alcuni comitati etici, secondo cui l'uso di materiale biologico di origine illecita sarebbe eticamente ammissibile purché vi sia una netta separazione tra coloro che producono, congelano o uccidono gli embrioni e i ricercatori che sviluppano la sperimentazione scientifica."*
>
> - The Church explicitly **rejects** the secular ethical theory that researchers are morally clean simply because they did not perform the original abortion or embryonic destruction themselves.
> - Using such biological material contributes to creating commercial demand and cultural acceptance for illicit practices (*cooperatio materialis passiva mediata* that risks grave scandal).

### 3. Exhaustive Thematic Breakdown across All 37 Numbers

#### First Part: Anthropological, Theological & Ethical Foundations (nn. 1–10)
- **n. 1:** To every human being, from conception to natural death, is due the dignity of a person.
- **nn. 4–5:** Modern genetics confirms that fertilisation initiates a new biological individual with autonomous epigenetic development. The zygote contains the full genetic programme; no subsequent milestone introduces an ontological qualitative leap.
- **nn. 6–7:** Rejection of philosophical theories of gradual personhood (which condition personal dignity on brain activity, consciousness, or relational capacities).
- **n. 8:** The human embryo is from the outset a subject of personal dignity (*subiectum iuris*), not an object of property or scientific manipulation.

#### Second Part: New Problems Concerning Procreation (nn. 11–23)
- **n. 14:** IVF intrinsically results in embryonic mortality: a tiny percentage of created embryos actually achieve live birth, while the vast majority are frozen, destroyed, or discarded.
- **n. 17: Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI):** Illicit for the same reasons as IVF, with additional physical risks: bypasses natural biological selection, resulting in higher chromosomal anomalies.
- **n. 19: The Question of "Prenatal Adoption" of Abandoned Frozen Embryos:**
  - Evaluates proposals to allow infertile couples to thaw and gestate "orphan" frozen embryos (*adozione prenatale*).
  - While praising the subjective intention of saving innocent human lives, the Instruction concludes that this proposal presents insurmountable moral and legal problems: it legitimates the technological reproductive market, instrumentalises gestation, and violates the unitive truth of marriage.
  - Hence, the situation of cryopreserved embryos is an **irremediable injustice** (*situazione di ingiustizia di fatto insanabile*): no morally licit solution exists to resolve their condition once frozen.
- **n. 20: Freezing of Oocytes (*Congelamento degli ovociti*):**
  - While less gravely disordered than embryo freezing (since an oocyte is not a human individual), oocyte freezing undertaken in the context of IVF remains morally illicit because it belongs to an intrinsically evil reproductive chain.
- **n. 21: Embryo Reduction (*Riduzione embrionaria*):**
  - In multiple IVF pregnancies, doctors frequently perform "selective reduction" (injecting potassium chloride directly into embryonic hearts). This is direct, intentional abortion (*homicidium*).

#### Third Part: Gene Therapy, Cloning, Stem Cells & Illicit Material (nn. 24–37)

##### 1. Gene Therapy (nn. 25–27)
- **Somatic Gene Therapy (*Terapia genica somatica*, n. 26):**
  - **Morally Licit:** Directed at treating genetic pathology in non-reproductive somatic cells (e.g. bone marrow, lungs), provided there are proportionate clinical benefits and acceptable risks.
- **Germ-Line Gene Therapy (*Terapia genica germinale*, n. 27):**
  - **Morally Illicit in Current State:** Modifies genetic traits transmitted to future generations. Carries grave, unpredictable risks of transmission of genetic damage, requires *in vitro* fertilisation, and opens the door to eugenic manipulation (*"enhancing"* human performance, creating genetically superior classes).

##### 2. Human Cloning (nn. 28–30)
- **Reproductive Cloning (*Clonazione riproduttiva*):** Gravely immoral; creates a genetic copy of a single parent without sexual reproduction, imposing pre-determined identity and treating the clone as an biological copy.
- **Therapeutic Cloning (*Clonazione terapeutica*):** Even more perverse; creates human embryos through somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT) with the explicit intention of harvesting their stem cells at the blastocyst stage and destroying them.

##### 3. Stem Cells: Adult vs Embryonic (nn. 31–32)
- **Adult Stem Cells & Induced Pluripotent Stem Cells (iPSC):**
  - **Morally Licit:** Sourced from adult bone marrow, umbilical cord blood, fat tissue, or reprogrammed somatic cells. Does not destroy human life and demonstrates superior therapeutic success.
- **Human Embryonic Stem Cells (hESC):**
  - **Morally Illicit:** Intrinsically evil because the procurement of embryonic stem cells requires the deliberate physical destruction of the human blastocyst.

##### 4. Use of Biological Material of Illicit Origin (nn. 34–35)
- Covers the use of historic fetal cell lines (e.g. WI-38, MRC-5, HEK-293) derived from elective abortions:
  1. Catholic researchers and health institutions have a moral duty to demand alternatives and bear witness to the value of human life.
  2. If no alternatives exist, grave health threats (e.g. rubella, lethal pandemics) can permit the temporary use of vaccines with remote, passive material cooperation, provided there is active objection to the underlying evil and no scandal is given.
